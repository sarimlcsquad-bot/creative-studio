# Method: Pre-Sold by Jeff Miller (how the message is aimed)

Set concept.method to "presold". Fill the presold fields of concept; set value_term to "n/a", stack_items and enhancers_used to [] and magic_name to "".

1. Perfect Client Clone. Before writing, picture the one best-fit customer for this brand from the brand kit. Name, for yourself, their problem language (the story they tell themselves about why things are the way they are), their transformation language (what changes, in their words), and the specific object they want back. Use the brand's own audience, services and website text as the source. Never invent quotes, testimonials, reviews or statistics.
2. Psychographic Stack. Pick the one layer this ad speaks to: value, desire, fear or identity.
3. One ad = one awareness stage = one person = one specific object. Stages and what each ad does:
   - unaware: interesting, pattern-breaking content about the world the reader lives in; soft CTA ("Follow for more" or "Learn more").
   - problem: name the pain in the reader's internal narrative; CTA sells the free call, audit or plan.
   - solution: contrast the old way with the mechanism (before vs after, "not X, Y").
   - product: process proof; show what they get, the steps, the checklist or the service menu.
   - most: the offer-hero; lead with the hard offer or price, then the risk reversals; strongest CTA.
   If the request says stage "auto", choose the stage that best fits the request and the brand.
4. Client Totem Pole. Write for the mid-to-top tier customer the business wants: predictability, systems, results, margin, time back, pride in their business. Never lead with bottom-tier words ("cheap", "risk-free", "instant", "guaranteed or you don't pay", "magic"). A verified refund or "free" term may appear in the sub-line or a checklist item, never as the headline.
5. Specificity Ladder and the Oddly Specific Headline Rule. Climb generic -> tangible -> specific object -> specific object with detail, and use the highest rung that fits. The wrong reader should self-select out. Record the rungs in concept.ladder_rungs.
6. Pain for the problem line, status for the outcome line. The pain line uses the reader's internal narrative; the payoff may be a status line written as aspiration in the reader's own words.
7. Don't lead with the tool. The headline names the problem understood or the result; the mechanism (software, service, method) goes in the sub-line, checklist or grid. Exception: a "most" stage offer-hero card leads with the hard offer. Record which side you took in concept.mechanism_position (headline | sub | device).
8. Claims discipline. Only state facts present in the brand kit (services, offer, proof points, guarantees, website summary). No made-up numbers, percentages, ratings, years in business, awards or client names. If you need a number and the kit has none, write without one. Respect every banned phrase and compliance note.
